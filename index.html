About the model. Each orbital is a hydrogen-like wavefunction ψ = Rnl(r)·Ylm(θ,φ) using real spherical harmonics, with the effective nuclear charge Z* from Slater’s rules, so sizes are comparable across subshells and elements. Lobes are the |ψ| isosurface enclosing the chosen share of electron density; the electron cloud is random points drawn from |ψ|². Bright and dark shades mark the positive and negative phase of ψ. Arrows in the boxes follow Hund’s rule.

How to read it. ψnlm = Rnl(r)·Ylm(θ,φ) for a one-electron atom with nuclear charge Z. n sets the size and energy, l the shape (s, p, d, f…), mₗ the orientation. Real orbitals mix +mₗ and −mₗ into the familiar px, dxy shapes, colored by the sign of ψ. Complex orbitals have a definite mₗ; |ψ|² is symmetric around z and the color wheel shows the phase, which winds mₗ times around the axis. Use Cutaway to see inner shells from radial nodes.
